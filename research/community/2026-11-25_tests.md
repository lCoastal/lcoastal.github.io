## Patel's autopan efficiency formula

```
rolls per second = Luck × 0.688 × Cap^0.563
                   ÷ ( ceil(Cap ÷ ShakeStr) ÷ (23.514 × ShakeSpd ÷ (442.88 + ShakeSpd))
                       + 1.15
                       + ceil(Cap ÷ DigStr) × 143.9 × DigSpd^-0.9324 )
```

- Items per pan (100% quality): 0.688 × Cap^0.563
- Shakes per second (fewer than 8 to 10 shakes to empty the pan): 23.514x ÷ (442.88 + x), x = Shake Speed %
- Time per dig (s): 143.9 × DigSpd^-0.9324
- Picture: [autopan-efficiency-formula.png](autopan-efficiency-formula.png)
