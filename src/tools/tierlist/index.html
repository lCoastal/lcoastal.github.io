Tier List

Rank minerals, equipment or runes. Drag them into a row, or tap one and then tap a row. Hold Ctrl and click (or press and hold on a phone) to grab several at once, then tap a row to move them together. Click a row's label to rename it, recolour it, move it or delete it.

One row per line, written as Row name: item, item. A first line without a colon becomes the list name. It works out minerals, equipment or runes by itself.

Tip: tap an item to pick it up, then tap a row to drop it. Hold Ctrl and click, or press and hold on a phone, to pick several at once.
