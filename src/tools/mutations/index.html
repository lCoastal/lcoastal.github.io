Gear Mutations

What each mutation does

A mutation multiplies every stat on the item. Some also add a flat bonus on top. Each item can only have one mutation, and it's kept when you reforge or merge to 6★.

Chance per roll at the Mutation Forge

The forge at Frozen Peak rerolls the mutation using the item's catalyst. Putting in more catalysts makes the better mutations more likely. These are the odds without any event running.

How to read this: these odds go by how many catalysts you put in, with no event running. Events can change them. A Prismatic Catalyst applies Prismatic directly, and a Festive Catalyst applies Festive.

Pan enchants

Enchant at the altar behind the Alchemist in Fortune River with Aurorite, Aetherite or Aetherium. A “1.2× Pan Luck” enchant only multiplies the pan's own luck, not your other gear. One enchant per pan.

Shovel enchants

Enchant at the altar in the Azuralite Oasis with Aetherite or Aetherium. Enchants marked “Seed of Light” only show up after you finish The Seed of Light quest there. Apart from Toughened, shovel enchants change how digging works instead of adding stats.
