5-Star Crafter

Pick what you're crafting, enter the weight and modifier of each ore you'll use, and it tells you your star rating and exactly what's missing for 5★. A modifier counts as a big weight multiplier, and a heavier base-weight ore reaches the mark more easily.

How it works

  • Every ore you put in gets a quality score from its weight and modifier. The item's star rating comes from the average quality of all its ores, so one great ore can make up for weaker ones.
  • A modifier multiplies the ore's weight before quality is worked out. Perfect counts as ×24, so a small Perfect ore can be worth more than a big plain one.
  • Each ore type has its own minimum weight and quality step per recipe. Heavier base-weight ores can grow bigger, so they reach high quality more easily.
  • "Lightest fix" is the single Perfect ore that closes the whole gap on its own, picked from whichever material needs the least weight.

The formula

Ore qualityQ = 1 + (W × M − MW) ÷ QS
Star ratingstars = floor(average Q), from 1 to 5
5★ needsΣ (W × M − MW) ÷ QS ≥ 4 × N
One ore at 5★W = (MW + 4 × QS) ÷ M
Lightest fixW = (R × QS + MW) ÷ 24
Max reachable weight15.5 × base weight × (1 + size boost ÷ 100)

W = ore weight (kg) · M = modifier multiplier · MW = minimum weight · QS = quality step · N = ores in the recipe · R = quality still missing.
