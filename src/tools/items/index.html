Potions & Totems

Every potion and totem: what it does, how long it lasts and where to buy it.
Potions can be switched on in the Stat Calculator's Boosts. Runes have their own page.

Totem bonuses add to your boost pile, so a 2× totem plus another 2× boost is 3×, not 4×. The Starcrusher shovel makes totems 25% stronger.

How trinkets work

A trinket is a pan accessory with its own perk. Open your Prospector Kit, go to Pans, and press the orange “Trinket” button under Equipped to pick one. Each pan holds one trinket, and you can swap it at any time.

Rune slots

Runes go in the Equipment section of the Prospector Kit. Levelling up unlocks more slots.
