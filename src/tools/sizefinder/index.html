Size Boost Finder

Pick a mineral and the weight you want, and it tells you the Size Boost you need: the least that makes it possible at all, and a recommended one where your chances are actually decent.

Heads up: the minimum is exact. The chances come from a fitted model, so they're an estimate, especially at very low or very high capacity.

Chance per mineral at each Size Boost

How it works

  • A mineral's weight is base weight × (1 + Size Boost) × a roll between 0.75 and 15.5, ×1.5 with the Solar Magnifier. How the rolls spread is shown on Weight Range.
  • Minimum: the Size Boost where even the heaviest roll (k = 15.5) only just reaches your weight: Size Boost = weight ÷ (15.5 × base × magnifier) − 1. At the minimum it's possible, but almost never happens.
  • Recommended: the Size Boost where at least the share you picked of that mineral comes out at your weight or heavier. More capacity makes heavy rolls more common, so it lowers the recommended boost but not the minimum.
  • The chance is per mineral of that kind you pan, not per pan.
