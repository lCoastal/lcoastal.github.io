Reforge Odds

Pick a piece of equipment and see your chance of rolling it perfectly (or to the roll % you want) each reforge, how many reforges that takes and what it costs. Reforges get more expensive every time.

There's a pity system that drastically increases your odds

The game raises your chances the more reforges you do without a good roll. This tool doesn't count it yet, so your real chances are much better, and the real number of reforges and cost much lower, than shown here.

How it works

  • Each stat rolls from its minimum up to its top value in fixed steps, and every step is equally likely. A stat that can land on 41 values has a 1 in 41 chance of its max. All stats have to max at once for 100%, so their chances multiply.
  • Stars raise the top, not the bottom. Each star above 1★ adds the stat's quality step to the top of the range, so higher stars have more possible values and a 100% roll gets rarer. 6★ adds one more step on top of 5★.
  • Roll % is counted as the average of where each stat landed in its range (0% at the minimum, 100% at the maximum). Stats with only one possible value are left out.
  • Reforge cost: the Nth reforge costs Price × (1 + 0.5 × N1.8), where N is how many reforges were done before it and Price is the item's crafting price, from community testing. Items bought with Candy or Ornaments are counted in that currency.
  • Crafting Mastery is counted as a raised floor: at 25%, no stat rolls below a quarter of the way up its range, and the values left are equally likely. Not confirmed yet.
  • Crafter's Luck is counted as rolling twice and keeping the better one, either for each stat on its own or for the whole item (the roll with the higher roll %).
