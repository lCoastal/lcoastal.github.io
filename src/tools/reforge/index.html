Reforge Odds

Pick a piece of equipment and see your chance of rolling it perfectly (or to the roll % you want) each reforge, how many reforges that takes and what it costs. Reforges get more expensive every time.

Goal

How it works

  • Each stat rolls between its minimum and maximum in fixed steps, and every step is equally likely. A stat that can land on 41 values has a 1 in 41 chance of its max. All stats have to max at once for 100%, so their chances multiply.
  • Stars don't change the odds. Each star above 1★ moves the whole range up, so a 5★ roll has the same number of steps as a 1★ roll.
  • Roll % is counted as the average of where each stat landed in its range (0% at the minimum, 100% at the maximum). Stats with only one possible value are left out.
  • Reforge cost: the Nth reforge costs Price × (1 + 0.5 × N1.8), where N is how many reforges were done before it and Price is the item's crafting price, from community testing. Items bought with Candy or Ornaments are counted in that currency.
  • Mastery and gamepasses aren't counted yet.
