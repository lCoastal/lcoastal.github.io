Settings

How every tool in the toolkit looks and behaves. Changes apply straight away and are saved in this browser.

Your stats

When sharing is on, tools that use these stats read and update the same values. When sharing is off, each tool uses its own inputs.

Use Your stats across toolsShare these values with all tools that use them.

Appearance

ThemeAuto follows your device or the app. Light and Dark always use that theme.
Accent colourThe colour of buttons, switches, links and highlights in every tool.
Colors
All colorsTurn off toolkit color styling while keeping artwork and icons in color. Your individual choices are saved.
Rarity colorsMineral and equipment rarity labels, borders and badges.
Equipment mutation colorsMutation labels, cards and picker options.
Mineral modifier colorsModifier names, badges and cards.
Stat colorsLabels and markers for stats like Luck and Capacity.
Event and limited-time colorsEvent categories and limited-time badges.
Build comparison colorsColors used to distinguish builds in Compare Builds.
Tier colorsCustom tier labels and backgrounds in Tier List.

Display

Compact viewTighter tables, cards and chips, so more fits on screen.
Short numbersShows big numbers in short form. On a computer, hover one to see the full number.
Decimal placesThe most decimals a short number shows. Trailing zeros are dropped.

Browsing

Nerdy stuffShow Research in the sidebar. You can always find its tools in search.
Remember scroll positionOpening a tool again takes you back to where you were in it.
Image buttonsWhat Save as image does in every tool. Copy puts the picture on your clipboard, ready to paste into Discord.
Recently viewed itemsHow many minerals, equipment, gear and runes the top of the menu remembers. Remove one with its ×.
Recent toolsHow many tools you used last show at the top of the menu.
Search shortcutOpens the search box from any tool. Use one key, or two pressed together.

To clear every saved build, checklist and setting, use Reset everything at the bottom of the menu.
