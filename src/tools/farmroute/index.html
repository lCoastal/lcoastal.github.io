Museum Farm Route

Paste your museum loadout and get a farming route: which zones to go to, in what order, what to collect at each one and when to move on.
Minerals that share a zone are grouped so you never farm the same place twice.

Heads up: the route is planned from estimates. Drop chances come from the in-game zone weights and modifier odds from my own test runs, but enchants, events, weather and item weight aren't counted, so your real grind can go faster or slower. About 1 in 3 runs take longer than the expected pans.

Your museum

Paste the text from the Museum Planner's Copy as text button, or type one line per rarity: Rarity: Mineral (Modifier), Mineral. Leave the modifier off if any version will do.

    How the route is worked out

    • For every mineral it works out your chance per pan in each zone it drops in, using the same luck model as Luck Odds (about √capacity minerals per pan) and the modifier chances from Modifier Odds. Luck isn't counted for modifiers: it changes their odds a little, but by how much isn't known yet, so it needs testing.
    • It then shares zones out so minerals that drop in the same place are collected together. A mineral moves to a slightly worse zone if you'll be there anyway, because it's then almost free.
    • "Expected pans" for a stop is the average number of pans to get every item on it, not just the first. Luck swings a lot, so about 1 in 3 runs take longer than this.
    • Tick an item when you get it and the route updates. Weight isn't counted: an item gives its full museum boost only at its max weight, so you may want a heavier one later.
    • Your stats and area settings are shared with the route on the Crafting Checklist.
