Price and museum check

Good to know

  • A mutation multiplies the mineral's sell price. A 2 kg Gold with Scorching sells for 2 × Gold's price per kg × 2.
  • In the museum, a mutated mineral adds a small boost on top of the mineral's own boost. How big depends on the mineral's rarity, from +0.005× for Common up to +0.08× for Exotic, per stat. Treasured counts double.
  • Modifier Boost (from pans, enchants and jewelry) raises how many minerals get a mutation and makes the rarer ones more likely. The Solar Magnifier and the Modifier Surge event help too.
  • The Purity rune gives every mineral the Purified mutation, which adds nothing. Event mutations only sell at 1× and give no museum boost.
