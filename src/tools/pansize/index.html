Pan Size

How many minerals a full pan gives at your capacity: the fewest, the average and the most.

Every full pan lands somewhere between the fewest and the most. Most pans sit in the lower two thirds of that range; the high end is rare.

Capacity you need

The formula

Fewest½ × √capacity
Averageabout 0.68 × capacity0.565
Mostabout 2.5 × √capacity
  • Doubling capacity gives about 1.4× the minerals, not 2×, so capacity helps steadily but with smaller gains the more you have.
  • Luck doesn't change how many minerals you get. Events and items that give extra items (like the Calibrator, Volcanic Eruption or the Fortune River events) come on top.
