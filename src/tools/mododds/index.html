Modifier Odds

Your chance of every ore modifier from your Modifier Boost and luck.
Built on the game's modifier weights and testing.

Heads up: this is a model fitted on testing, not the game's own code, so it can be a little off. Some sessions come out higher than it says for a reason that isn't known yet. Enchants, events and weather aren't counted. How it works is explained at the bottom.

Next to each share, ▲ means more Modifier Boost makes that modifier more common and ▼ means it gets rarer. Hover one to see why.

How this is worked out

  • Does an ore get a modifier at all? How many ores get a normal one (Shiny up to Perfect) depends only on your Modifier Boost (luck makes no difference): more boost always helps, with smaller gains the more you have: about 7% at 113%, 32% at 600%, 45% at 926% and 66% at 1,660%. Some sessions at the same boost came out noticeably higher, so something else (likely weather or a zone effect) can raise it further. Voidtorn (from Void tools) and event modifiers like Ghostly or Spooky come on top of that.
  • Which one? Every modifier has a weight in the game: Shiny 1, Pure 0.8, Glowing 0.5, Scorching 0.25, Irradiated 0.1, Iridescent 0.02, Cosmic 0.005, Mutated 0.001, Lunar 0.00025 (night only), Perfect 0.0001. With no boost you'd simply get each one in proportion to its weight.
  • What boost and luck do: they work like luck does for minerals. The game rolls the modifier several times and keeps the rarest one it hits, so more rolls means rarer, better modifiers, not more of them. Both Modifier Boost and luck add rolls, boost much more so, and there's no cap on either. A Modifier Surge doubles the boost part.
  • Not every ore gets the full strength: about 31% roll just once, 13% at about a sixth of the strength and 56% at full strength. That split is what makes the model match testing.
  • Treasured only comes from treasure chests, Voidtorn from Void tools or The Void, Electrified from Thunderstorms and Crystalline from geodes or Crystal Touch, so they aren't listed. The Purity rune removes modifiers entirely. The Solar Magnifier isn't counted until it's been tested.
