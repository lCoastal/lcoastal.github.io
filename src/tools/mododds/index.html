Modifier Odds

Your chance of every ore modifier from your Modifier Boost.
Estimates built on the wiki's stat guide and its community drop counts.

Not exact: these are estimates built from community drop counts and a simplified model. They don't account for everything, like enchants, events, weather, Size Boost or hidden game rules, so your real odds can be quite different. Luck isn't counted. It does change modifier odds a little, but by how much isn't known yet, so it's left out until it's been tested.

How this is worked out

  • Effective Modifier Boost = your panel Modifier Boost × 2 for a Solar Magnifier × 2 for a Modifier Surge.
  • Which modifier you get starts from the game's modifier weights (Shiny 1, Pure 0.8, Glowing 0.5, Scorching 0.25, Irradiated 0.1, Iridescent 0.02, Cosmic 0.005, Mutated 0.001, Lunar 0.00025, Perfect 0.0001): each one's chance is its weight ÷ the total. Luck isn't counted. It does change modifier odds a little, but by how much isn't known yet, so it's left out until it's been tested.
  • How many ores get a modifier rises with effective Modifier Boost and tops out at about 99.4% around 1,700–1,900. The Solar Magnifier only helps here.
  • Modifier Boost works like luck does for minerals: the game rolls the modifier several times and keeps the rarest one. The strength is 14.5 × your Modifier Boost (shown in game as ×, so 597% = 5.97× → 87). About a third of ores roll once, a quarter at half strength and the rest at full strength. This was fitted on two test datasets (5.97× and 8.97×, about 214,000 minerals) and matches both within a few percent, so treat it as a good estimate rather than exact.
  • Treasured only comes from treasure chests, Voidtorn from Void tools or The Void, Electrified from Thunderstorms and Crystalline from geodes or Crystal Touch, so they aren't listed. Purity rune removes modifiers entirely.
