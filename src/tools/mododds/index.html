Modifier Odds

Your chance of every ore modifier from your Modifier Boost and luck.
Estimates built on the wiki's stat guide and its community drop counts.

Not exact: these are estimates built from community drop counts and a simplified model. They don't account for everything, like enchants, events, weather, Size Boost or hidden game rules, so your real odds can be quite different.

How this is worked out

  • Effective Modifier Boost = your panel Modifier Boost × 2 for a Solar Magnifier × 2 for a Modifier Surge. Luck adds a little too: up to +500, from min(50, luck ÷ 1000) × 10.
  • How many ores get a modifier rises with effective Modifier Boost and tops out at about 99.4% around 1,700–1,900. The Solar Magnifier only helps here.
  • Which modifier you get follows the community's measured shares for Perfect, Mutated, Cosmic and Iridescent, interpolated between test points. The common modifiers fill the rest in their usual proportions. Beyond the highest test point (≈6,700) shares are held where they were measured.
  • Treasured only comes from treasure chests, Voidtorn from Void tools or The Void, Electrified from Thunderstorms and Crystalline from geodes or Crystal Touch, so they aren't listed. Purity rune removes modifiers entirely.
