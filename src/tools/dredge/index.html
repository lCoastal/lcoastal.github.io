Dredge Master Planner

Type in the three things the Dredge Master wants and how many of each.
It finds where to pan and what luck cap to use, keeping as much as possible in one place.

Heads up: this runs on the same luck model as Luck Odds, with drop chances from the in-game zone weights. Modifiers, Size Boost, enchants, events and weather aren't counted, so take the numbers as a guide rather than a promise.

What the Dredge Master wants

Only minerals with known drop rates can be planned. Leave a row empty if he asked for fewer things.

The plan

How this is worked out

  • Every zone and every luck cap below your luck (plus no cap) is tried. A pan holds about √capacity minerals, so you find about √capacity × chance of a mineral per pan.
  • When several things are farmed in the same place they come in at the same time, so that stop takes as long as its slowest item.
  • Every way of grouping the items is compared: all in one place, two stops, or one stop each. If you turn on walking between zones, each extra stop adds the walking time you set. Either way, if fewer stops is within 15% of the best, fewer stops is picked so you don't have to keep moving.
  • The Void is only used when an item doesn't drop anywhere else, because its loot pool changes.
