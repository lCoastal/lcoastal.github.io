Luck Odds

Your chances of finding each mineral with your luck.
Built on the in-game zone weights and a luck model fitted on my own test runs.

⚠ THE LUCK FORMULA IS STILL AN ESTIMATE
It's fitted on my own test runs, not the game's real formula. It matches most minerals closely, but some can still be off, especially the rarest ones. Treat the numbers here as a guide, not exact odds. Every test is in the Test Log.

Heads up: each mineral's base chance comes straight from the in-game zone weights; how luck turns those into rolls is fitted on logged data. Shovel enchants that change drops (like Mythical), events other than the luck ones above, and weather aren't counted.

How this is worked out

  • Each mineral slot in a pan rolls four ways: about 49% of the time at 1.74× your luck, 19% at about 0.4× your luck, 3% at about 1/23 of your luck, and 29% as if your luck were 1. That's why commons never disappear. Fitted on about 2.3 million logged minerals across 11 zones, from 5,000 to 266,000 luck.
  • Very rare minerals, with base odds between about 1 in 18 million and 1 in 1.5 billion (most Mythics), show up about 1.86× more often than that roll model alone gives, so their chance is multiplied by up to 1.86.
  • At a given luck, the rarity roll is the best of that many tries, so rarer ores gain share as luck grows. The base rates are the drop chances listed for each location.
  • On average a pan holds about 0.68 × capacity0.565 minerals (measured: about 20 at 400 capacity, 44 at 1,600, 87 at 5,462), which is how per-pan chances are worked out. Minerals with exactly the same odds share their chance evenly.
  • Shovel enchants that change drops (like Mythical) and zone events aren't counted. Size Boost only changes how heavy minerals are (see Weight Range), not which ones you find.

The formula

For one zone, list its minerals from rarest to most common, with drop shares p₁, p₂, …, pₙ (as fractions that add up to 1). For the mineral in spot k:
