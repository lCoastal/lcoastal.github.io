Luck Odds

Estimated chances of finding each mineral with your luck.
These are estimates built on how the wiki describes luck, not exact game numbers.

Heads up: each mineral's base chance comes straight from the in-game zone weights, but how luck turns into rolls is a model, not the game's own code. Modifiers, Size Boost, enchants, events and weather aren't counted, so your real odds will be close but not exact.

Pan

How this is worked out

  • Each mineral slot in a pan rolls three ways: half the time at your full luck, a quarter of the time at half your luck, and a quarter of the time as if your luck were 1. That's why commons never disappear.
  • At a given luck, the rarity roll is the best of that many tries, so rarer ores gain share as luck grows. The base rates are the drop chances listed for each location.
  • On average a pan holds about √capacity minerals, which is how per-pan chances are worked out.
  • Size Boost, modifiers and enchants that change drops (like Mythical) aren't counted. Treat these as rough guides.

The formula

For one zone, list its minerals from rarest to most common, with drop shares p₁, p₂, …, pₙ (as fractions that add up to 1). For the mineral in spot k:

Everything rarera = p₁ + p₂ + … + pₖ₋₁
Up to and including itb = a + pₖ
Best of L rollsf(L) = (1 − a)L − (1 − b)L
Per mineralP = ¼·f(1) + ¼·f(max(1, L ÷ 2)) + ½·f(max(1, L))
Per pan (capacity C)1 − (1 − P)√C
