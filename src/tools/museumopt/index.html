Museum Improver

Checks every combination of minerals and modifiers for a museum that's better than yours without lowering any stat.
If there is one, it lists the swaps. If not, it shows the trades worth making. Values assume each mineral is at its max-boost weight.

Your museum

One rarity per line, the same as Copy as text in the Museum Planner.

Limits

Modifiers you can get

Treasured only goes on minerals from treasure chests or the tomb, and Entombed only on tomb minerals. Feather of Ma'at is always Treasured.

What counts as better

Every stat has to stay the same or go up. Among those museums, this picks the one with the most gain: every stat counted by how much it grows compared to what you have, or one stat first.

Pick your museum and press Find improvements.
