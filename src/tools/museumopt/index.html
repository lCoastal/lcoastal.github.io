Museum Improver

Checks every combination of minerals and modifiers for a museum that beats yours. By default nothing drops, but you can prioritise the stats you care about and allow others to fall.
If there's a better setup it lists the swaps. If not, it shows the trades worth making. Values assume each mineral is at its max-boost weight.

Your museum

One rarity per line, the same as Copy as text in the Museum Planner.

Limits

Modifiers you can get

Treasured only goes on minerals from treasure chests or the tomb, and Entombed only on tomb minerals. Feather of Ma'at is always Treasured.

What counts as better

Prioritise — click in order

It maxes out your first pick, then the next, and so on. Leave empty to improve every stat your museum boosts, evenly.

Can afford to lose (how far each stat may drop, in ×)

By default nothing drops. Allow a stat to fall by up to the amount you set to unlock bigger gains elsewhere.

Pick your museum and press Find improvements.

How it works

  • Your totals are the floor. It adds up your museum the same way the Museum Planner does (each mineral's boost plus its modifier, at max-boost weight). A new museum has to reach every one of those totals.
  • What's allowed. Only the modifiers you ticked, with the game's rules: Treasured only on treasure-chest and tomb minerals, Entombed only on tomb minerals, Feather of Ma'at always Treasured. Minerals you marked as not having are left out. Whatever is on your board now always counts as possible.
  • What "better" means. Every stat counts by how much it grows compared to what you have, so +0.1 on a small stat counts more than +0.1 on a big one. Prioritise to rank which stats matter, and use Can afford to lose to let a stat drop. Keeping minerals you already have gets a tiny bonus, so it never swaps more than it needs to.
  • Cutting the search down. Options that can never win are dropped first: a modifier that's beaten by another on the same mineral, a mineral beaten by at least as many others as that rarity has displays, and any set of minerals for a rarity that another set beats on everything.
  • The search. It then picks one set per rarity with an exact optimiser: it works out the best any museum could possibly do, splits the problem into smaller cases, and skips any case that can't beat the best museum found so far. When nothing is left to check, the answer is proven best. This usually takes under a second.
  • No improvement? Then it tries every single swap, one display at a time, and lists the ones with the biggest overall gain together with what each one costs.
  • Limits. Weights are assumed to be at max boost. With lots of modifiers ticked it only checks the strongest options per rarity, and a search that runs past about 9 seconds shows the best it found; in both cases it tells you the answer isn't proven best. The trades only look at one swap at a time.
