How many minerals and luck rolls a build gets per pan and per minute, and which stat would speed it up most.

Delays

0 extra per dig is a perfect setup with no ping. Macros add some: one community test came out about 0.3 s slower per dig. The fixed time covers walking into the water and the pan settling; 1.15 s is the community's figure.

How it works

Rolls per panLuck × minerals per pan
Rolls per minuterolls per pan × 60 ÷ time per pan
  • Speeds are in percent, as the Stat Calculator shows them (100% is base speed).
  • Time per dig is from Patel's dig test (159% to 603% Dig Speed, within 5% of every point). Above about 600% it hasn't been tested. Shakes per second is from a community test at 6 shakes per pan; pans that need many more shakes may be slower.
  • Minerals per pan is fitted from our test logs; the community's own fit (0.688 × Capacity0.563) is within 1% of it.
  • Rolls is a score for comparing builds, not a real count: luck doesn't raise rare odds in a straight line, and each zone has a luck cap. For how many pans a mineral takes, use Luck Odds or Compare Builds.
  • No ping, no walking between deposits, no selling. Real play is slower; the order of builds stays the same.
