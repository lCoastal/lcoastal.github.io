Good to know

  • Speeds are percentages, as the Stat Calculator shows them: 100% is base speed.
  • Dig time is from Patel's dig test, within 5% of every point from 159% to 603% Dig Speed. Faster than about 600% hasn't been tested.
  • Shake rate is from a community test at 6 shakes per pan. Pans that need many more shakes may be slower.
  • Minerals per pan is fitted from our test logs. The community's own fit, 0.688 × Capacity0.563, is within 1% of it.
  • Rolls is a score for comparing builds, not a real count. Luck doesn't raise rare odds in a straight line, and each zone has a luck cap. For how many pans a mineral takes, use Luck Odds or Compare Builds.
  • Best case: no ping, no walking between deposits, no selling. Real play is slower, but builds stay in the same order.
