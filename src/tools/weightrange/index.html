Weight Range

How heavy a mineral can come out with your Size Boost and capacity: the lightest, typical and heaviest weight, and how often each size shows up. Pick a zone instead to see every mineral there at once.

Heads up: the lightest and heaviest weights are exact. How often each weight shows up comes from a fitted model, so it's an estimate, especially at very low or very high capacity.

How it works

Weightbase weight × (1 + Size Boost) × roll
Rollbetween 0.75 and 15.5
  • Lightest and heaviest: every mineral falls between 0.75 and 15.5 × base weight × (1 + Size Boost).
  • Capacity matters: about a quarter of minerals always come out small, near the lightest weight. The rest are spread evenly at low capacity, and the bigger your pan, the more of them lean towards the heavy end. The heaviest possible doesn't change.
  • What doesn't matter: luck, the modifier, which mineral it is and where in the pan it lands make no difference.
  • Size Boost as a number: 1,476% is 14.76. The Solar Magnifier is ×1.5 on the final weight, and a Volcanic Eruption doubles sizes in its zones.
