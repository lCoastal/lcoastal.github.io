Best Luck Trinket

Type your stats and it ranks the four luck trinkets — Nomad's Compass, Senet Die, Beach Umbrella and Mass Reactor — by how much luck each one gives your build.

Heads up: trinket perks are estimated the same way as the rest of the toolkit — the formulas aren't the game's exact ones. The Senet Die is random (×0.5 to ×3 a pan), so it's shown as an average with its range. The ranking compares each trinket's average luck.

How each trinket makes luck

The maths

Ores per pann = 0.678 × capacity0.565
Modified ores per pann × (1 − 0.937mb/100)
Umbrella / Reactor+25 base luck per modified / destroyed ore
Nomad's Compass×(1 + 0.15 × stacks)
Senet Die×0.5 to ×3 (average ×1.33)

Because the additive trinkets add to base luck, their boost is worth more when your base luck is low; the multiplier trinkets (Nomad, Die) grow with whatever luck you already have. That's why the answer depends on your stats.
