How stars, quality and mutations work

Stars set the stat range (0★ uses the same range as 5★), quality is where the roll lands inside it (0% worst, 100% best), and the mutation multiplies the result on top.
This is a planner, so quality is spread evenly over every stat. In game a 50% item isn't always 50% on each stat: it could be 100% luck and 0% sell boost, which still averages out to 50%.

Stats breakdown

Every stat is flat points × a multiplier. Flat points come from your pan, shovel, rings and potions; the multiplier starts at ×1 and museum displays, totems and other boosts add to it. Each line is what that one source adds. Click a source to count only that part. Pick which stats show here with the Breakdown switches in Your stats.
