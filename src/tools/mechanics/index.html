How It Works

How the game picks your minerals, modifiers and weights, as far as testing shows.
Change the numbers in each box to see what they do. These are the same models the rest of the toolkit uses.

⚠ THE LUCK FORMULA IS STILL AN ESTIMATE
It's fitted on testing, not the game's real formula. It matches most minerals closely, but some can still be off, especially the rarest ones. Treat the numbers here as a guide, not exact odds. Every test is in the Test Log.

Heads up: the game doesn't publish any of this. Everything here is worked out from testing and the wiki, so treat it as a very good guess, not the game's code. Some parts are still being tested, and the luck formula is not exact.

Luck: which mineral you get

Every zone has a drop table. With no luck, each mineral comes up at its listed chance. Luck works like extra rolls: the game rolls the table several times and keeps the rarest result. So luck doesn't add a flat bonus; it pushes every pick towards the rare end of the table, and common minerals get rarer as a result.

The best fit so far is that about 29% of minerals roll just once, about 3% roll roughly luck ÷ 23 times, about 19% roll about 0.4 × luck times and about 49% roll about 1.74 × luck times, keeping the rarest result. On top of that, minerals with base odds between about 1 in 18 million and 1 in 1.5 billion (most Mythics) come up about 1.86× more often than the rolls alone predict. That's why the gain slows down: going from 100 to 1,000 luck matters far more than 10,000 to 11,000 for anything that isn't very rare.

The wiki's Stat Systems Guide uses a simpler split (a quarter once, a quarter at half luck, half at full luck). Testing fits the split above much better, especially for mid-rarity minerals like Jade and Onyx and for very rare ones at high luck.

Luck caps give less than they say. A capped session seems to behave like about 63% of the cap, while uncapped sessions match the luck shown. Best Luck Cap and Dredge already account for this.

Events change your luck too. A Meteor Shower doubles your final luck and weather events (Nimue's Blessing, Blizzard, Swamp Fog, Starfall) multiply it by 1.5, and they stack. Hallows' Eve, the Luck Totem and most other boosts add to your multiplier instead. Autopan without the Mastered enchant digs at 51% quality, which gives about two thirds of your luck. Best Luck Cap, Dredge and Luck Odds have a setting for events and autopan.

For a mineral that's the rarest in its zone. Something with rarer minerals above it gains less, because those take the rare rolls first.

Capacity: how many tries per pan

A full pan doesn't give one roll per slot. It behaves like about 0.68 × capacity0.565 separate picks on average, so 400 capacity is about 20 picks, 1,600 is about 44 and 5,462 is about 87. Each pan lands somewhere between ½ × √capacity and about 2.5 × √capacity. Doubling capacity gives roughly 1.4× the picks, which is why capacity helps steadily but never explodes. The Pan Size tool shows yours.

Modifiers: how often and which one

How many minerals get a normal modifier rises with Modifier Boost only: about 1 − 0.937boost (×), so roughly 7% at 113%, 32% at 600%, 45% at 926% and 66% at 1,660%. Modifier Boost also changes which modifier you get. It works like luck does for minerals: the game rolls the modifier several times by its weight and keeps the rarest. The roll strength is about 11.7 × boost (×)0.84 + 2 per 1,000 luck (no cap), with 31% of rolls once, 13% at about a sixth of the strength and 56% at full strength.

Voidtorn, Treasured, Electrified and other event modifiers come from their own sources and sit on top of that.

At very high Modifier Boost the formula keeps climbing (about 80% at 2,500%), but that end is less certain.

Weight: what's known so far

  • Every mineral has a base weight (shown on its Minerals page). Its weight is base weight × (1 + Size Boost) × a roll between 0.75 and 15.5.
  • About a quarter of minerals always roll small, near the bottom of that range. The rest spread across it, and the bigger your capacity, the more of them lean towards the heavy end. Weight Range and Size Boost Finder use this.
  • Luck doesn't change weight, and neither does the modifier or which mineral it is.
  • Size Boost is a straight multiplier on (1 + Size Boost). The Solar Magnifier is ×1.5 on the final weight.
