How much does each stat matter?

0 ignores a stat. A stat on 2 counts twice as much as one on 1. Each stat counts by how many times bigger it gets, not by its raw number, so stats with big numbers (like luck) don't drown out small ones (like walk speed), and the more you already have of one, the less extra counts.
